Heroes, listen. I built this tower stone by stone, and for what? So some farm boy with a shining sword can climb up, lecture me, and lose. I have conquered nine kingdoms, cursed three moons, and still nobody brings me tea before noon. My generals plot. My ravens unionised. The dungeon leaks. The dragon moved to a warmer cave and took my best cloak. So come, swing your little sword. Win, if you must. I will be here, in this draughty chair, ruling the dark, waiting for someone — anyone — to somehow finally fix the blasted leaking roof, please.
